Tips to prevent bites

The most important step in the fight against ticks is prevention. Choose an effective tick prevention product all year round; there are plenty of options depending on your pet's lifestyle, such as the Seresto™ collar or Advantix™. Great sources of information for tick prevention and general pet health can be found on the official Elanco website.

For proper information about tick bites and prevention, consult a healthcare professional or your local health authority.
